### Root cause
The root cause is the absence of effective safeguards around governance execution. A proposal that granted broad fund or token-transfer rights was allowed to reach and complete execution. No timelock delay, elevated quorum requirement, or automated flagging of high-risk actions prevented the outcome.

### Why it matters
Governance systems on Solana (and elsewhere) routinely hold large treasuries. When a single proposal can move those funds without additional friction, the attack surface is reduced to capturing enough votes or exploiting low participation. The $21.3 million loss demonstrates that this vector remains viable even for established protocols.

### Takeaways for protocol teams
- Enforce a mandatory timelock (minimum 24–48 hours) on any proposal that touches treasury assets or privileged roles.
- Require elevated quorum or veto thresholds for actions that grant token-transfer rights, mint authority, or ownership changes.
- Implement automated proposal scanning that flags and pauses execution of any call pattern matching broad fund movement or role escalation.
- Until these controls are in place, treat every governance vote as carrying direct custody risk.
