Former New York Governor Andrew Cuomo, now a director at crypto exchange OKX, told CNBC on August 6 that lawmakers need to pass the Clarity Act before Congress leaves for August recess. He described the bill as essential for setting clear regulatory boundaries and called the process “highly political.”

Cuomo said Democrats and Republicans remain locked in disagreement primarily over the ethics provisions. The House passed its version last year. A new draft circulating this week adds bipartisan changes to the ethics section that would bar government officials from promoting or profiting from crypto. The White House is reviewing the revised language.

## Partisan Friction Over Trump Ties

Cuomo linked the Senate delay to Democratic efforts to raise the Trump family’s crypto businesses during the campaign. He pointed to criticism of meme coins and the World Liberty Financial protocol while noting the White House maintains no conflicts of interest exist. Cuomo added that Democrats “have to be a little careful” when discussing conflicts of interest given their own stock-trading practices.

Cuomo stated on Squawk Box: “We need clarity… Tell me the rules — I want to play the game fairly, but you have to tell me the boundaries, and that’s what the Clarity Act is all about.” He also said he believes the Senate will resolve the ethics issues and pass the bill, adding that “the world is passing us by.”
