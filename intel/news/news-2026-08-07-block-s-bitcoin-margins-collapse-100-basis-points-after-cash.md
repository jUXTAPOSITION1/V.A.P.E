Block’s total gross profit rose 25% to $3.166 billion in the second quarter, while its Bitcoin Ecosystem gross profit fell 31% year over year to $72 million. Commerce Enablement gross profit rose 18% and Financial Solutions gross profit rose 43%, according to the company’s Q2 shareholder letter.

Bitcoin Ecosystem revenue fell about 13% to $1.894 billion from $2.172 billion a year earlier. The category’s implied gross margin narrowed to about 3.82% from 4.84%, a compression of roughly 102 basis points. Revenue declined roughly $278 million while gross profit fell about $33 million.

Block attributed the gross-profit decline to a strategic decision to reduce fees on certain Cash App bitcoin transactions and to bitcoin trading dynamics. The company did not quantify either factor’s contribution. The filing provides no Bitcoin-specific activity data such as transaction volume or user counts.

Cash App announced on Feb. 9 that it was lowering transaction fees and removing fees and spreads entirely for bitcoin buys over $2,000. Block’s Q2 filing referred more broadly to lower fees on “certain” transactions.

A related Feb. 27 report noted that Jack Dorsey has called for cutting Block’s workforce by up to 50%, from more than 10,000 employees to under 6,000, with projected restructuring charges of $450 million to $500 million.
