## What Happened

VanEck’s spot Bitcoin ETF, the VanEck Bitcoin Trust (HODL), is officially ending its fee waiver. Starting August 1, 2026, a 0.20% sponsor fee will apply across all of the fund's assets. 

The fee waiver was originally designed to remain in place until the fund reached a specific growth target, but the incentive has concluded with HODL falling approximately $1.4 billion short of that milestone. The fund currently holds $1.076 billion in assets.

## The Details

With $1.076 billion in assets under management, the 0.20% sponsor fee is projected to generate roughly $2.15 million in annual sponsor fees for VanEck, assuming asset levels remain flat. 

At this new 0.20% fee level, HODL's pricing will:
* Match Bitwise's Bitcoin ETF (BITB)
* Undercut BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT)
* Cost one basis point (0.01%) more than Franklin Templeton's Franklin Bitcoin ETF (EZBC)
