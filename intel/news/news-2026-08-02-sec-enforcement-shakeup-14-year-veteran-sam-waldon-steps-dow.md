The Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) has announced the departure of Sam Waldon, the Principal Deputy Director of the Division of Enforcement, effective July 31, 2026. Waldon will leave after more than 14 years at the agency. He will be succeeded by Osman Nawaz, who previously served with the SEC from 2010-2024 before rejoining the agency last month.

## A Career Spanning More Than Fourteen Years

Waldon served as Acting Deputy Director from October of his tenure. 

SEC Chairman Paul S. Atkins praised Waldon’s service, stating that his contributions “are reflected in the many key leadership roles he has taken on during his tenure.” Atkins added, “I have personally benefited from Sam’s wise counsel and appreciate everything he has done for our agency. I wish him the best in all of his future endeavors.”

Director of Enforcement David Woodcock said, “Our paths have crossed multiple times at the agency – most recently while Sam has served as the Principal Deputy. His work ethic and steadfast commitment to the Division of Enforcement have been invaluable and his mentorship to countless colleagues will be sorely missed.”

## Nawaz Steps Into the Role

Osman Nawaz will assume the role of Principal Deputy Director. Waldon stated, “I will be forever grateful to Chairman Atkins and Commissioners Peirce and Uyeda for the opportunity to work under their leadership and for their commitment to the agency and its mission. To my colleagues in the Division of Enforcement, I cannot put into words how proud I have been to have had the privilege of working with you. Over the last several years, I have had a front row seat to witness your hard work, talent and dedication under incredibly challenging circumstances – it has been nothing short of inspirational. And finally, I want to thank David and Os for their friendship and stewardship of the Division. I will miss working with you both, but I take great comfort in knowing that Enforcement has never been in better hands.”
