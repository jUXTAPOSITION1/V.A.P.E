# Coinkite Warns Coldcard Mk3 Users of Potential Fund Risks Amid 594 BTC Theft Reports

**Hardware wallet manufacturer Coinkite has issued an alert to Coldcard Mk3 users following reports of a major bitcoin theft.**

Coinkite has issued a warning to users of its Coldcard Mk3 hardware wallet, stating that "funds may be at risk." The alert follows reports of a significant theft involving approximately 594 BTC. (Source: [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/410235/coinkite-coldcard-mk3-warning))

Details regarding the exact nature of the security risk, the vulnerability exploited, or how it relates to the reported 594 BTC theft have not been publicly disclosed. 

Coldcard Mk3 users are advised to remain vigilant, monitor their wallet activity, and watch for official updates and security recommendations from Coinkite.
