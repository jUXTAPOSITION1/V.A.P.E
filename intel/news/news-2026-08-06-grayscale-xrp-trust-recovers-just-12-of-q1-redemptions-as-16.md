Grayscale’s XRP Trust (GXRP) clawed back 480,000 shares in the second quarter, yet that rebound covered only 12.2% of the 3.94 million-share contraction recorded in Q1. The Aug. 4 Form 10-Q filing shows the partial recovery was immediately offset by $16.846 million in operating losses, driven almost entirely by investment declines.

The six-month picture remains sharply negative. GXRP issued 1.87 million shares and redeemed 5.33 million across the first half, producing a net contraction of 3.46 million shares. Q2’s positive flow replaced roughly one of every eight shares lost earlier, leaving 87.8% of the Q1 outflow still unrecovered at June 30.

## Share-Flow Details from the Filing
The quarter split cleanly between creations and redemptions. Issuances totaled 510,000 shares while redemptions reached 30,000. Those movements added $12.743 million in net capital after accounting for $13.442 million received from new shares and $699,000 paid out on redemptions.

Earlier reports of a broader $165 million six-month reduction in fund value align with the cumulative effect of repeated outflows plus investment losses. The filing does not break the full-period loss into quarterly components beyond the Q2 figure.

## Investment Losses Outweigh Capital Inflows
Operating results turned negative despite the share rebound. The $16.846 million decline from operations included a $16.789 million investment loss: $16.327 million from the drop in unrealized appreciation, $433,000 in realized losses on XRP sold to meet redemptions, and $29,000 in realized losses on XRP sold for expenses. A separate $57,000 net investment loss completed the total.

Net assets fell to $57.413 million from $61.516 million at the end of March. Holdings stood at 55.036 million XRP on June 30, up 20.2% for the quarter but still 55.0% below the year-end balance.
