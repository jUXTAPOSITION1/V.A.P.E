## The Announcement at Rare Evo

Binance.US plans to apply for a Designated Contract Market (DCM) license with the United States Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) in August 2026. The announcement was made by Binance.US CEO Steve Gregory on Wednesday, July 29, during the Rare Evo blockchain conference. 

If approved, the DCM license would allow the cryptocurrency exchange to offer prediction markets services
