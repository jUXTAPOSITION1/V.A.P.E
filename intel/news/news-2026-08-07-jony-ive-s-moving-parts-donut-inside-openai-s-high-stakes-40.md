OpenAI is planning to enter the consumer hardware market with a premium, "donut-shaped" AI smart speaker priced between $300 and $400, according to a new report from Bloomberg cited by TechCrunch. The device, designed in collaboration with Jony Ive’s firm LoveFrom, is described as the “physical manifestation” of ChatGPT. It is scheduled for a likely 2027 release.

The reported price point places the device well above most Amazon Echo models, which range from $40 on the low end to $240 on the high end. Bloomberg’s sources describe the donut shape as intentional, allowing users to carry the unit between rooms and set it on surfaces such as kitchen counters or bedside tables. The exterior is expected to be made of “high-quality metal” with a “premium look,” and the device will include distinct “moving parts,” though their function is not specified.

OpenAI’s hardware effort has already encountered legal friction. Apple is suing the company, alleging theft of trade secrets; OpenAI has denied the claims. The TechCrunch report notes that smart-speaker hardware has historically been difficult to make profitable and that a high price could further complicate market entry.
