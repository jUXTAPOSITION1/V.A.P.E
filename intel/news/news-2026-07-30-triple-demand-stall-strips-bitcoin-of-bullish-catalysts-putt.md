Bitcoin is once again testing the resilience of its market structure as it hovers near the critical $64,000 support level. The current market setup reveals a stalling of demand across multiple capital channels. 

According to on-chain analysis and market flow data compiled by [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/three-key-demand-drivers-stall-at-once-leaving-bitcoins-64000-support-to-long-term-holders/), three key demand drivers in the digital asset ecosystem have stalled simultaneously, leaving the asset heavily reliant on the conviction of long-term holders.

## The ETF Exodus: Institutional Capital Retreats

The most immediate drag on Bitcoin’s price has been the reversal of flows in US spot Bitcoin ETFs. Over four trading sessions, these investment vehicles registered a combined $526.5 million in net outflows. 

## Speculative and Organic Capital Flatlines

Beyond the institutional wrapper, the native crypto markets are exhibiting a parallel loss of momentum. On-chain intelligence firm Glassnode has identified weaker perpetual buying and stagnant broader on-chain capital inflows as the other two stalled demand drivers. 

With these primary demand sources sidelined, long-term-holder conviction remains the main buffer as Bitcoin tests support near $64,000.
