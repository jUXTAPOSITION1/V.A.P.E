The Securities and Exchange Commission will convene a public roundtable on September 17, 2026, at its Washington headquarters to discuss moving towards 24-hour trading in the U.S. equity markets. The event will focus on preparations to support overnight trading, operations and resiliency in a 24-hour market, and the opportunities and challenges of market expansion. No other dates or preliminary findings have been released.

## Core Details From the Announcement
The roundtable is listed under File Number 4-913 and will be streamed live on SEC.gov, with a recording made available at a later date. In-person attendance may be limited, and visitors will be subject to security checks. Public comments can be submitted electronically through the SEC’s internet form or via email to rule-comments@sec.gov, provided they reference the file number and are submitted using only one method to avoid duplicate entries.

Chairman Paul S. Atkins stated: “We are moving towards a new day – and night – in the U.S. equity markets.” He added that the expansion to overnight trading would seek to align U.S. equity markets with those that already trade continuously while balancing the shift with “all-important investor and customer protections.”

Holding the session in mid-September gives staff roughly eight weeks to finalize an agenda and speaker list, both of which remain unpublished.
