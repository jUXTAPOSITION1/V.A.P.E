According to Hong Kong police data, 25 romance-linked fraud cases were reported between July 24 and July 30, 2026. 

## The $3.3 Million Romance Trap
The most severe case reported during the week involved a Hong Kong insurance agent who was fleeced of $3.3 million. The victim was targeted on a dating application by a scammer posing as a potential romantic partner. Over several weeks, the perpetrator built a deep psychological rapport with the agent before introducing the concept of cryptocurrency investing.

The scammer directed the victim to a fraudulent trading application designed to mimic a legitimate digital asset exchange. To encourage larger and more frequent deposits, the platform displayed fabricated profits, leading the victim to believe her investments were highly successful. The illusion shattered only when she attempted to withdraw her funds and found her account permanently locked.
