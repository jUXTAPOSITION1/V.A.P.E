## What Happened
US-listed spot Bitcoin ETFs attracted $172.4 million in net inflows across July, according to SoSoValue figures reported by Cointelegraph. This marked a reversal after nearly $7 billion in combined outflows during May and June, including a record $4.5 billion exit in June alone. The positive monthly result came despite a volatile close to the period, when funds logged the $265.4 million daily outflow on the final Friday.

Year-to-date flows stayed negative at roughly $5.3 billion. Only March, April, and July produced inflows, totaling $3.46 billion, while the remaining months posted $8.75 billion in outflows.
