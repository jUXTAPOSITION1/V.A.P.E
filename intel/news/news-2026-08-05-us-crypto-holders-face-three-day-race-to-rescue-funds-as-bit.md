United States cryptocurrency investors are facing a short window to secure their assets as offshore exchange BitMart prepares to restrict accounts under new compliance rules. According to an urgent notice issued on August 5, 2026, U.S. residents and those physically located in the country must close all trading positions, cancel outstanding orders, and withdraw their funds by August 8 at 23:59 UTC. This regional deadline is earlier than the exchange's broader global wind-down schedule.

The notice applies to any individual who resides in or is physically located within the United States, as well as any user otherwise classified as a U.S. person under BitMart’s terms of service and internal compliance policies. Affected users are instructed to cancel outstanding orders, close applicable positions, redeem or withdraw eligible product balances, and remove their remaining assets.

After the August 8 cutoff, BitMart reserves the right to impose further restrictions on non-compliant accounts. Any assets left on the platform will be handled in accordance with applicable law, the BitMart user agreement, and the exchange's compliance procedures.

## Withdrawal Reviews

Withdrawal requests may be subjected to identity verification, source-of-funds information, proof of ownership for the destination wallet, or a security review. The exchange has not provided a maximum processing time for these checks.

## Global Timeline

BitMart plans to halt all spot, futures, and other trading services globally on August 26, 2026, at 01:00 UTC. The exchange recommends that users withdraw remaining balances by August 26 at 05:00 UTC. Platform operations are scheduled to end on January 31, 2027.

## BitMart U.S.

BitMart U.S. is a separate platform that requires a new account and its own identity checks. Eligibility depends on the user's jurisdiction, and users must confirm that their assets, networks, and intended services are supported.
