## What Happened

Quantum has executed a significant liquidation of its digital asset treasury, selling 1,000 ETH to generate $1.903 million in proceeds. The transaction, however, came at a financial cost, resulting in an expected loss of JPY 17 million (approximately $108,000 USD at current exchange rates). 

This sale is part of a broader corporate restructuring strategy aimed at liquidating up to 4,375 ETH to fund the company’s transition into the AI Data Center (AIDC) sector. While the company’s board has authorized the sale of an additional 2,471 ETH to complete this funding campaign, Quantum has run into a major operational hurdle: a "collateral wall." 

Of the company's remaining Ethereum holdings, only 1,714.8 ETH are currently unpledged and available for immediate sale. The remaining balance required to fulfill the authorized 2,471 ETH divestment is locked up as collateral in active financial positions, restricting the company's ability to liquidate them without first unwinding those underlying obligations.

## Key Statements

In explaining the roadmap for the remainder of its divestment plan, Quantum emphasized that its execution strategy remains flexible and highly dependent on external variables. According to the company, further ETH sales will depend on "prices, market conditions, AIDC progress and funding needs."
