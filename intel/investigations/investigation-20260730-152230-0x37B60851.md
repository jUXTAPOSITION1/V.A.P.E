This target is a bridged representation of the original SUSHI governance token (from SushiSwap on Ethereum) that arrived on Avalanche in late August 2020. The combination of a 2162-day pricing history on an independent aggregator, 5927 holders, and a non-factory verified contract named BridgeToken points to a standard cross-chain lock-and-mint implementation rather than a fresh deployment. The mintable flag is therefore expected behavior for any bridge-wrapped asset, but the absence of an owner address removes the most common rug vector (an EOA that could still call mint).
