## Expert Assessment
- Agrees with the verdict above:

The evidence paints PUPPY as a long-dormant meme token that launched on Uniswap more than 2.5 years ago, reached a verified custom contract state, then had its ownership fully renounced with liquidity permanently locked. That combination explains the clean GoPlus flags (no mint, no taxes, no honeypot) and the absence of any rug event across nearly 1,000 days of trading. At the same time, the on-chain picture is one of near-total illiquidity and extreme centralization: only 90 wallets hold the token, the top nine already control 86 % of supply, and the entire pool sits at roughly $4.5 k. The result is a contract that is technically “safe” from the usual rug vectors yet functionally unusable for ordinary trading—any meaningful buy or sell would move the price dramatically or simply fail to execute.

The rule-based score correctly flags the concentration and liquidity risks but under-weights the protective effect of the 972-day clean history once renouncement and lock are confirmed; those two facts together make an exit-scam scenario far less plausible than simple abandonment. Conversely, the score does not appear to over-weight the lack of audit or team identity, because the same evidence set shows no on-chain activity that would require a team in the first place.

Next step: pull the top-nine holder addresses and run a multi-month transaction graph to determine whether those wallets are dormant early buyers or actively coordinated.
