The evidence gathered on the NOX token presents a mixed picture, with both reassuring and concerning signals. On one hand, the token has a reasonably distributed holder base with 9823 holders, deep liquidity of over $1 million, and has been trading for over 134 days without any known incidents. This suggests a certain level of maturity and stability. Additionally, the fact that a market-data aggregator has priced this token for over 123 days provides independent corroboration of its longevity. 

However, there are also significant red flags. The top 10 non-LP/burn holders control 100% of the supply, which is a highly concentrated and easily manipulable situation. Furthermore, only 0% of the liquidity is locked, meaning the deployer can pull the rest at any time, posing a significant risk to investors. The token-safety data does show some positive signs, such as no honeypot, buy or sell tax, and it's not mintable, but the lack of liquidity locking and the concentrated supply are major concerns.

From my own background knowledge, I recognize that a token with such a long trading history and deep liquidity without incidents could indicate a legitimate project, but the concentration of supply and lack of liquidity locking are unusual for a project of this size and age. Typically, projects that have been around for this long and have such a large holder base would have more distributed supply and locked liquidity to prevent manipulation.

The rule-based verdict of CAUTION (70/100) seems to be underweighting the risks associated with the concentrated supply and lack of liquidity locking. While the positive signals are significant, the potential for manipulation and the deployer's ability to pull liquidity at any time are substantial risks that could outweigh the benefits. 

A concrete next step for investigation would be to examine the on-chain transactions and wallet interactions of the top 10 non-LP/burn holders to understand their behavior and potential relationships with the deployer. This could provide insight into whether these holders are independent or if they are, in fact, connected to the project team, potentially exacerbating the concentration risk.
