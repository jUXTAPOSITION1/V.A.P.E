## Expert Assessment

The evidence paints CAP as a recently deployed (roughly one month old) upgradeable proxy token on BSC that has attracted a surprisingly large holder base of ~6700 while maintaining zero buy/sell taxes and decent 24h volume relative to its tiny liquidity pool. The custom-verified ERC1967Proxy implementation plus the holder count together suggest an attempt at a legitimate distribution rather than a pure one-off honeypot or rug template, yet this is directly undercut by the extreme concentration (top-10 non-LP wallets holding 93 % of supply) and the complete absence of any locked liquidity. Those two facts together imply that whatever distribution created the broad holder list left effective control in very few hands that can still move the market or drain the remaining $11.5 k pool at will. No on-chain or market data here identifies an actual product, team, or utility, so the “project” reduces to a bare token with trading activity but no visible fundamentals anchoring it.

The rule-based score correctly flags the proxy and concentration risks but under-weights the liquidity-unlock exposure because the 0 % locked figure is paired with an already minuscule pool; a single large holder can remove the entire market-making capacity without needing to control 93 % of the token supply itself. Conversely, the score slightly over-weights the “many holders” positive because the same data set shows those holders are largely powerless against the top-10 concentration.

Next step: pull the actual implementation contract address behind the ERC1967Proxy and inspect its owner/admin functions plus any recent upgrade events to determine whether control has already been renounced or remains with the same wallets that dominate the top-10 holdings.
