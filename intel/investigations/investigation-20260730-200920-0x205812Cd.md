## Expert Assessment
- Agrees with the verdict above:

The evidence points to a BeaconProxy token on BSC positioned as a high-priced tracker ("Invesqo QQQ") trading against a deep PancakeSwap pool. The combination of verified custom source code, zero taxes, substantial reported volume, and 3001 holders initially suggests a functional synthetic or leveraged product rather than a quick meme. However, the same data set reveals a structural mismatch: 91% of supply sits with the top 10 non-LP addresses while liquidity remains fully unlocked and the contract is upgradeable with no visible owner. This pattern is inconsistent with organic distribution; the holder count and volume figures can coexist with centralized control if the dominant wallets are routing trades among themselves or providing the apparent depth. The 30-day age further limits any claim that the project has survived meaningful market stress.

The rule-based score overweighted the generic "pair age" penalty while underweighting the concrete contradiction between the 3001-holder narrative and the 91% concentration metric; those two data points cannot both be true in a meaningfully decentralized way. It also treated the upgradeable proxy as a standalone risk rather than connecting it directly to the unlocked liquidity, which together create a single point of failure the deployer can still exercise.

Next cycle or human review should pull the actual top-10 holder addresses and map their first-interaction timestamps and common counterparties to test whether they form a coordinated cluster.
