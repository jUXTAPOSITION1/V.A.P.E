This is the native governance token of Pangolin, Avalanche’s primary native DEX, which launched in early 2021 and has maintained continuous price history on DefiLlama since 19 Feb 2021. The on-chain data line up with that identity: the contract is verified with custom (non-factory) source, carries no mint or proxy functions, reports zero owner, and trades exclusively on the Pangolin pair itself. The 38 k holders and modest but persistent daily volume are consistent with an established protocol token rather than a short-term meme or exit-scam vehicle.

The concentration metric (top-10 non-LP wallets holding 87 %) and the complete absence of locked liquidity are real and material. In a younger token those two facts together would normally signal high manipulation risk; here they are tempered by the project’s age and the fact that Pangolin’s treasury and staking contracts are known to sit among the largest holders. Still, without public confirmation that those large wallets are time-locked or multisig-controlled by the DAO, the distribution remains a structural vulnerability that the 38 k-holder count alone does not neutralize.

The rule-based engine correctly flags the two hard risks but under-weights the corroborative power of the 1 983-day DefiLlama track record; that external pricing feed is independent of the token’s own contracts and would be difficult for a malicious deployer to fabricate over such a span. Conversely, the “no pair timestamp” penalty is overweighted once the DefiLlama first-seen date is taken into account—it supplies the missing temporal baseline.
