## Expert Assessment
- Agrees with the verdict above:

This token launched under 24 hours ago on PancakeSwap as an anonymous, custom-verified contract with no minting capability or proxy. Ownership is renounced to the zero address and the entire liquidity pool is locked, which removes the classic dev-controlled rug vector. However, the on-chain footprint shows only 13 wallets holding the entire supply, with the top 10 controlling 100 % outside the LP. That same tiny $2.5 k pool has already recorded over $800 k in reported 24 h volume—an order-of-magnitude mismatch that cannot be explained by organic retail flow given the holder count. The combination points to either coordinated wash trading among the concentrated wallets or fabricated volume metrics rather than a functioning market.

The rule-based score correctly flags the concentration and extreme newness as primary risks; those factors are not overweighted because they directly enable the volume anomaly. What is under-weighted is the liquidity-to-volume ratio itself: once the locked LP and renounced ownership are acknowledged, the remaining threat model shifts from “dev steals the pool” to “small group can still manipulate price and reported activity with almost no external participants,” a distinction the raw score does not isolate.

Next check should be a wallet-level breakdown of the 24 h trades to determine whether the reported volume originates from repeated interactions among the same handful of addresses or from a broader set of counterparties.
