The combination of renounced ownership, fully locked liquidity, zero mint capability, and a non-factory custom contract forms a coherent anti-rug skeleton that matches the on-chain signals (verified source, zero taxes, no proxy). At the same time, the 5-day pair age plus 53% supply in the top 10 wallets creates a clear tension: the holder count of 1,004 looks distributed on paper, yet the concentration allows a handful of early wallets to dictate price action in an $18k liquidity pool that has already seen 1.45× daily volume turnover. The 22% price drop in that window is consistent with early profit-taking rather than organic growth, and the absence of any identifiable team or external audit leaves the project reliant solely on the on-chain locks for credibility.

The rule-based score overweighted the raw age and liquidity metrics (standard for any new meme launch) while underweighting the specific holder-concentration risk; 53% in ten wallets is not merely "meaningful" but structurally dangerous when liquidity is this thin, because even modest coordinated sells can empty the pool without touching the locked portion. Conversely, the score may have underweighted the custom verified source, which reduces the likelihood of hidden backdoors compared with mass-deployed templates.
