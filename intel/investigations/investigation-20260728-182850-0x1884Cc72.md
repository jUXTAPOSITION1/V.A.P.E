## Expert Assessment
- Agrees with the verdict above:

The evidence paints AIPF as a low-activity Polygon token branded "AI POWERED FINANCE" that has survived 120+ days on Uniswap without an overt rug or honeypot event. The combination of 15,450 holders, zero buy/sell taxes, and non-zero liquidity (~$163k) creates an initial impression of a distributed community asset, yet this is directly undercut by the fact that the top 10 non-LP wallets still hold the entire supply and the contract is an upgradeable proxy with no visible owner. That concentration-plus-upgradeability pairing means any appearance of broad distribution is illusory; the same small group that controls 100 % of tokens can also alter the logic at will, and the unlocked liquidity gives them an immediate exit path. The near-zero 24 h volume ($0.81) further suggests the project is effectively dormant rather than actively used, so the holder count may largely reflect long-abandoned wallets rather than ongoing participation.

The rule-based score correctly flags the structural risks but under-weights the liquidity figure itself: an unlocked pool of that size on a low-volume token is not merely a theoretical danger but a practical one, because any single large holder can drain it without needing to coordinate with others. Conversely, the 120-day clean history is overweighted; longevity on a chain with cheap transactions does not offset the absence of locked liquidity or renounced control, especially when the proxy pattern has no on-chain implementation details supplied in this cycle.

Next step should be to pull the actual implementation address behind the proxy and inspect its source for mint, pause, or ownership-transfer functions that could still be live.
