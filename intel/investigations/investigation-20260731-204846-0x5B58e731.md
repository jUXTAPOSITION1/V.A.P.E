## Expert Assessment
- Agrees with the verdict above:

This token's on-chain footprint points to a classic ultra-fresh Uniswap meme launch that executed the standard "renounce + lock" trust play within its first hours, then immediately experienced a violent retail-driven or coordinated pump. The 0.4-day pair age, sub-$35k liquidity, and 7698% 24h swing together indicate the move was almost entirely speculative momentum rather than any underlying utility or adoption; the 671-holder count and renounced ownership (zero-address) plus fully locked liquidity are consistent with that playbook, as they remove the most obvious rug vectors while still allowing early wallets to dominate price action. The 125% top-10 concentration (non-LP/burn) directly undercuts the "reasonably distributed" narrative and suggests a small set of wallets—possibly coordinated or simply the deployer and first snipers—still control the float despite renouncement. The contract itself being custom-verified rather than a clone is a modest positive signal of intent, but does not change the fact that the entire setup is only hours old and therefore untested against sustained trading or external scrutiny.

The rule-based score overweighted the "pausable by owner" flag, which is now inert because the owner address is the zero address; that risk item should carry zero weight post-renounce. It also under-weighted the holder-concentration metric relative to the extreme recency of the pair—those two data points together are far more predictive of manipulation risk than the raw liquidity number alone.

Next cycle or a human reviewer should pull the top-10 holder addresses and map their transaction graphs for shared funding sources or synchronized sell walls.
