This token launched on PancakeSwap less than a day ago under the on-chain name QToken (despite the marketed ticker Beat). The combination of zero liquidity, a 24-hour volume figure that is already larger than any remaining pool, and a -100% price move points to liquidity having been added and then fully removed in the same short window. Zero taxes, no mint function, and no owner address are technically clean, yet they sit alongside a holder base of only 24 wallets where the top 10 already control 78% of supply; that distribution is consistent with a small group that can coordinate dumps or wash trades once any new liquidity appears. The rule-based score correctly flags the extreme concentration and unlocked liquidity as primary risks, but it underweights the market data showing liquidity already at zero: that single datapoint turns the “fresh launch” risk into evidence of an active or completed exit rather than mere potential for one.
