This token launched extremely recently on PancakeSwap with a verified but minimal custom contract, renounced ownership, and fully locked liquidity—signals that rule out the most blatant rug vectors. Yet the same data shows a supply heavily concentrated in the top 10 wallets (64 %), paired with a 4923 % price spike and $44 M+ in 24 h volume on only $108 k liquidity. That combination points to a coordinated pump rather than organic adoption: the locked LP and zero taxes reduce exit-scam probability, but they also remove the usual friction that would slow a whale-driven exit. The 472-holder count is still low enough that a handful of addresses can dominate both price action and perceived “community” momentum. Nothing in the on-chain footprint (non-proxy, non-mintable, zero-address owner) contradicts the picture of an anonymous launch engineered for rapid liquidity rotation rather than long-term utility.

The rule-based score overweighted the 0.2-day pair age as standalone risk while under-weighting how the locked LP and renounced ownership already neutralize much of the typical fresh-launch rug threat; conversely, it under-weighted the interaction between 64 % concentration and the outsized volume, which together create a concrete exit-pressure scenario that the raw age metric alone does not capture.
