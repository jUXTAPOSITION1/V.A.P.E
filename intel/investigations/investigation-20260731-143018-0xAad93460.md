## Expert Assessment
- Agrees with the verdict above:

The evidence paints GRVT as a classic ultra-fresh BSC memecoin launch that achieved rapid traction on PancakeSwap but shows multiple structural red flags that undermine any claim of organic legitimacy. The 0.6-day pair age directly explains the +2364% move and 465k volume against only 51k liquidity: this is not sustained trading but a single explosive entry window, during which the top-10 non-LP wallets already accumulated 54% of supply. Renounced ownership and zero-address owner plus non-mintable code remove the most blatant rug vectors, yet the complete absence of locked liquidity leaves the remaining pool fully removable by whoever added it. The custom verified contract (4.2kB, non-proxy, zero taxes, non-honeypot) is a modest positive that rules out obvious factory-copy scams, but it does not offset the concentration-plus-unlocked-LP combination that lets early wallets exit while the deployer can still drain the pool at any moment. Taken together, the picture is coherent only as a high-risk launch with built-in exit pressure, not as a project with credible staying power.

The rule-based score correctly weights the fresh-pair and unlocked-liquidity risks but may slightly overweight the 54% concentration without confirming whether those wallets are exchange hot wallets, known snipers, or a single coordinated group; the 269-holder count alone does not resolve that. It also underweights the renounced-ownership signal relative to the unlocked LP, since renouncement is meaningless if the liquidity itself remains the controllable attack surface.

Next cycle or a human reviewer should pull the exact transaction that added the current liquidity and trace its originating wallet against the top-10 holders to test for overlap.
