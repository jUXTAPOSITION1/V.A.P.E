## Expert Assessment
- Agrees with the verdict above:

This token is a 0.9-day-old Uniswap pair trading under the exact ticker and branding of the established Uniswap governance token (UNI), yet deployed at an unrelated address with its own custom ERC20 implementation. The combination of zero taxes, no mint capability, renounced ownership, and 296 holders initially looks like a standard fair-launch meme, but that picture collapses against the unlocked liquidity, sub-$30k pool size, and 2900% single-day swing on $424k volume: these metrics together describe a low-float vehicle that can be (and almost certainly is being) manipulated by whoever still controls the LP tokens. The verified source code is the only mitigating detail, showing someone bothered to write and publish a non-templated contract, yet that effort does not extend to locking liquidity or providing any on-chain or off-chain identity, leaving the project functionally indistinguishable from a classic pump-and-dump or soft-rug setup.

The rule-based score correctly flags the dominant risks but under-weights the name collision itself; impersonating a top-50 token on the same DEX is not a neutral branding choice but an active deception signal that amplifies the fresh-pair and unlocked-LP warnings far more than a generic low-liquidity launch would. It also slightly over-weights the lack of a third-party audit, which is routine for tokens of this age and size and adds little incremental information once the LP and volatility data are already in view.

Next step: pull the exact transactions that seeded the UNI/WETH pool and trace the deployer wallet(s) forward to see whether any large holder or LP-controller address has already begun distributing or whether the liquidity remains entirely in one or two wallets.
