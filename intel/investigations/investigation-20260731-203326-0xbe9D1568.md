## Expert Assessment
- Agrees with the verdict above:

The evidence points to a SpaceX-branded token launched as a BeaconProxy on BSC roughly seven weeks ago. The combination of 95k+ holders with deep reported liquidity and volume creates an initial appearance of organic traction, yet this sits in direct tension with the ownership concentration (top-10 non-LP wallets holding 96 % of supply) and the complete absence of locked liquidity. Those two facts together imply that the apparent distribution is largely illusory—most holders likely control negligible fractions while a small set of wallets retains effective control and the ability to drain the pool at any moment. The fact that the identical deployer previously shipped QQQB, which received its own REJECT verdict, strengthens the inference of a repeatable pattern rather than an isolated project. The verified custom source and zero taxes remove some low-effort red flags, but they do not offset the structural control risks embedded in the proxy architecture and unlocked liquidity.

The rule-based score correctly flags the proxy and serial-deployer issues but may be under-weighting the ownership concentration signal; 96 % control by ten wallets is extreme enough that even a modest coordinated sell or liquidity removal would collapse the market, regardless of headline holder count. Conversely, the score slightly over-weights the “custom verified source” positive because the BeaconProxy pattern itself allows the logic to be swapped post-deployment, rendering the current verified code only a snapshot.

Next concrete check: pull the creation transaction and any subsequent admin calls on the proxy to determine whether the implementation contract has already been upgraded since launch and whether the same EOA that deployed QQQB retains upgrade rights.
