## Expert Assessment
- Agrees with the verdict above:

This token has operated for roughly five months with steady external corroboration (DefiLlama pricing history, 45k+ holders, consistent Uniswap volume in the mid-five figures), and its verified contract shows no minting capability, no proxy, and zero fees—reducing the most common rug vectors. Those facts cohere into a picture of a live, non-trivial project rather than a quick-exit template. However, the same data set reveals a structural mismatch: 98 % of supply sits with the top ten non-LP wallets while the owner address remains fully privileged. That combination means the apparent distribution across 45k holders is largely cosmetic; a handful of entities can still move the market or extract liquidity at any time, and the modest $31 k pool amplifies any such move. The 147-day incident-free run is therefore less reassuring than it first appears—it simply shows the controlling wallets have not yet chosen to act.

The rule-based score correctly flags both the unrenounced owner and the concentration, but it under-weights how little the “no mint / no tax” signals actually constrain the owner once the contract is examined. Without the source functions or event logs, we cannot tell whether the owner retains pause, blacklist, or fee-setter rights that could be activated tomorrow; longevity alone does not close that gap.

Next concrete check: pull the verified source and any owner-only functions, then trace the top-ten holder addresses for inbound/outbound patterns over the last 30 days to see whether they behave like a coordinated group or independent market participants.
