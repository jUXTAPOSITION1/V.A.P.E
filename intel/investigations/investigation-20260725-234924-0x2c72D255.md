## Expert Assessment
- Agrees with the verdict above:

The evidence paints AIPF as a roughly six-month-old token that has achieved meaningful on-chain traction (deep Uniswap liquidity, sustained DefiLlama pricing history, 36k+ holders, renounced ownership, and fully locked LP) while still carrying two structural red flags that have not been resolved by time or distribution. The combination of a custom verified contract, zero buy tax, negligible sell tax, and no honeypot flags suggests a project that launched with at least some intent to operate as a tradable asset rather than an immediate exit scam. However, the 99 % top-10 concentration sits in direct tension with the large holder count: the bulk of supply is still controlled by a handful of wallets even after nearly 250 days of trading, which undercuts any narrative of organic retail diffusion. Mintability remains live per GoPlus despite the zero address owner, implying the mint function was never removed from the contract itself; this leaves a latent dilution vector that renunciation alone does not close.

The rule-based CAUTION score correctly flags the mint and concentration issues but appears to overweight raw holder count as a mitigating factor. A 36k-holder figure loses much of its reassurance when 99 % of tokens sit in ten addresses; that distribution pattern is more consistent with a small set of early or affiliated wallets than with broad, healthy dispersion. Conversely, the score may underweight the longevity signal: 180 days of independent pricing on DefiLlama plus locked liquidity and renounced ownership together reduce the probability of a classic rug even if the token remains manipulable by its largest holders.

Next cycle or a human reviewer should pull the top-10 holder addresses and check whether any are known exchange cold wallets, vesting contracts, or simply dormant since launch; that single on-chain lookup would clarify whether the concentration is custodial or adversarial.
