## Expert Assessment
- Agrees with the verdict above:

The evidence paints a picture of a low-activity, upgradeable token that has persisted in a minimal state for roughly four months without apparent collapse or heavy promotion. The 1336-holder count and DefiLlama pricing history together suggest some organic (if tiny) trading community formed early and has not fully exited, while the zero-tax, non-honeypot profile and verified custom proxy code indicate it is not a one-click factory scam. However, these longevity signals sit in direct tension with the structural facts: an ERC1967Proxy with no visible owner, essentially all liquidity still controlled by the deployer, and top holders showing an over-100% concentration that implies either massive overlap or the ability to move supply at will. The reported Uniswap venue on BSC chain 56 adds a further data-layer inconsistency that prevents the on-chain and market pictures from aligning cleanly.

The rule-based score overweighted the raw concentration and liquidity-lock numbers without sufficiently discounting the possibility that the 103% figure is an artifact of how non-LP wallets are tallied in a proxy setup; conversely, it underweighted the four-month DefiLlama track record, which is harder to fake for an entirely abandoned or malicious contract.

Next cycle should pull the proxy's implementation address and its current admin slot to determine whether upgrade rights are actually burned or merely unclaimed.
