## Expert Assessment
- Agrees with the verdict above:

The evidence describes a low-cap BSC meme token ("The Final Form Cow") deployed 25 days ago with zero taxes, no minting capability, and a renounced zero-address owner. Liquidity is fully locked and the contract is a modestly sized (3.8 kB), non-proxy, custom-verified deployment rather than a factory clone. These elements together reduce classic rug vectors and imply at least some deliberate setup by the deployer. However, the same data set shows only 87 holders, negligible 24 h volume ($125), and top non-LP/burn wallets controlling the entire supply. That concentration in such a thin market makes price discovery and any coordinated accumulation or dump trivial, regardless of the locked LP. The picture is therefore internally consistent with an anonymous, low-effort meme that has taken basic anti-rug steps but has not achieved (or perhaps even attempted) organic distribution or external validation.

The rule-based score correctly weights the concentration and liquidity risks; it does not appear to overweight the age or audit flags, both of which are directly supported by the on-chain timestamps and absence of any linked identity. The one element that may be under-weighted is the combination of renounced ownership plus 100 % locked liquidity: while these do not offset the holder skew, they do materially change the threat model from “deployer can drain” to “small cabal of early wallets can manipulate,” a distinction worth separating in future scoring.

Next concrete check: pull the top-10 non-LP holder addresses and map their first-in and subsequent transfer patterns to determine whether they share funding sources or act in concert.
