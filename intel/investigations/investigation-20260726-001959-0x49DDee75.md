## Expert Assessment
- Agrees with the verdict above:

The evidence paints EBCC as a mid-aged Polygon token (233+ days live) whose verified contract shows clean mechanics—no minting, zero taxes, renounced ownership at the zero address, and no proxy—yet whose on-chain distribution and liquidity setup remain structurally fragile. The 4610-holder base and absence of any reported exploit during that window suggest organic trading activity rather than a quick rug, but this is undercut by the fact that the top nine non-LP wallets still hold the entire circulating supply; that concentration, paired with fully unlocked liquidity, means a small set of actors could still coordinate dumps or drains without needing contract-level privileges. The market footprint (roughly $170 k liquidity, $53 k daily volume) is modest and consistent with a low-profile token that never achieved broad DeFi integration, but the mismatch between “reasonably distributed” holder count and total supply control by nine addresses creates an internal contradiction the scan itself flags.

The rule-based CAUTION score correctly weights the two hard risk vectors (concentration + unlocked LP) but underweights the long clean trading history; 233 incident-free days on a renounced contract with real volume is stronger evidence of non-malicious intent than the raw holder count alone implies, and the 0 % locked liquidity is less alarming when the contract itself cannot be altered to enable a honeypot or fee change.

Next cycle or a human analyst should pull the top-ten holder addresses and cross-check whether any overlap with the original deployer or with the current liquidity-provider wallets, then verify whether those same addresses have moved tokens in or out of the pool during the last 30 days.
