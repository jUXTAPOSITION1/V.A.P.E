The combination of a renounced zero-address owner, zero mint capability, fully locked liquidity, and zero buy/sell taxes produces a contract that can no longer be altered by any single party; the 4231-holder base and continuous DefiLlama pricing feed further indicate it has survived the typical early rug window without incident. The main internal tension is the 66 % supply held by the top ten non-LP wallets. While the overall holder count looks distributed, that concentration level means a handful of entities could still exert outsized sell pressure or coordinated dumps even though they no longer control the contract itself. Liquidity of ~$140 k against $45 k daily volume is thin enough that any such move would be immediately visible on-chain, but the data set supplies no wallet-label or historical-flow information to judge whether those top holders are exchanges, long-term believers, or dormant team-adjacent addresses.

The rule-based 92 score correctly weights the renounced/locked/longevity positives but under-weights the concentration risk because it treats the 4231-holder figure as an automatic mitigator; in reality the two metrics are not independent—high concentration can persist inside a large holder list if the remaining tokens are spread across many dust wallets.

Next concrete check: pull the top-ten holder addresses and run a multi-month transaction graph to determine whether they show coordinated timing, shared funding sources, or repeated large transfers to exchanges.
