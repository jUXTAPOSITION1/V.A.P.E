## Expert Assessment
- Agrees with the verdict above:

This token launched on PancakeSwap roughly nine days ago with a verified, non-proxy contract that explicitly disables minting and sets near-zero taxes. Ownership is already at the zero address and nearly all liquidity is locked, which together remove the classic post-launch rug vectors. At the same time the entire circulating supply sits in the top ten non-LP wallets, liquidity is only ~$10 k, and 24-hour volume is under $6. The 23 k holder count therefore cannot reflect meaningful distribution; it is consistent with a handful of large wallets plus thousands of dust or bot addresses that never trade. The combination produces a picture of a deliberately “safe-looking” low-cap token that has been abandoned or never gained traction rather than an active project.

The rule-based score correctly flags concentration and youth but under-weights the near-total absence of trading activity. With liquidity this thin and supply this centralized, even locked LP does not prevent a single large holder from controlling price; the low volume simply makes any movement invisible to standard monitors until it is too late.

Next step: pull the top-ten holder addresses and their transaction histories to determine whether they are independent or show coordinated funding patterns.
