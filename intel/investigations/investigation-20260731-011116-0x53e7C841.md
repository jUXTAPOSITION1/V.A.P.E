## Expert Assessment
- Agrees with the verdict above:

This token presents as a bare-minimum custom ERC20 deployment with zero taxes, renounced controls, and verified but non-templated source—yet the on-chain footprint is a textbook concentrated launch. The three-holder structure holding 100 % of supply, paired with a Uniswap pair that is less than half a day old and showing only single-digit dollar volume, indicates the entire float is still inside the wallets that created it. No liquidity figure is reported because essentially none exists in any meaningful depth; the contract therefore functions more like a private allocation vehicle than a tradable asset. Nothing in the data connects this to any identifiable team, website, or prior project history, so the “custom verified” signal is the only non-negative data point and is outweighed by the total absence of distribution or usage.

The rule-based score correctly weights the extreme holder concentration and launch age as primary red flags; it does not appear to overweight them. What it may under-weight is the combination of “no owner” plus “liquidity = none”: even if ownership is formally renounced, the three controlling wallets can still move or withhold the entire supply at will, rendering the renouncement cosmetic until actual liquidity and external holders appear.

Next concrete check: pull the creation transaction and the first few transfer events to map exactly which addresses received the initial mint and whether any of those wallets have interacted with other tokens or shown prior on-chain behavior.
