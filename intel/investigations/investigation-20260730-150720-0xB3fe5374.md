## Expert Assessment
- Agrees with the verdict above:

This token presents as a Chainlink (LINK) representation on Avalanche via Pangolin, with a price that tracks the real asset and a first-seen timestamp aligning with Chainlink's 2017 launch. However, the combination of an unverified contract, explicit mintability, owner-controlled pausability, zero locked liquidity, and extreme concentration among the top 10 wallets (64%) points to a low-effort clone or wrapper rather than an official bridged deployment. The 438 holders and zero taxes are consistent with a thin trading vehicle, but the $3.6k liquidity pool and negligible volume make any price discovery meaningless and leave the entire float exposed to a single liquidity pull. The long DefiLlama pricing history is the only point that superficially suggests legitimacy, yet it can be explained by an aggregator simply indexing any ticker that matches "LINK" without contract-level verification, creating a false continuity signal that conflicts with the on-chain red flags.

The rule-based score correctly weights the mint/pause/liquidity risks as dominant; it does not appear to overweight them, because those features directly enable the exact dilution and rug vectors that the tiny pool size already makes trivial to execute. The only element potentially under-weighted is the mismatch between "owner=None" in the safety scan and the simultaneous claim that transfers are pausable by an owner—suggesting either an incomplete ownership transfer or a proxy pattern not captured by the scan.

Next step: query the Avalanche explorer directly for any prior ownership transfers or mint events on this exact contract, then cross-reference the resulting address(es) against Chainlink's published Avalanche deployment list.
