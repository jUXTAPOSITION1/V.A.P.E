## Expert Assessment
- Agrees with the verdict above:

The evidence paints AKE as a long-dormant, low-effort token that reached a modest holder base early on but has since collapsed into near-total illiquidity. The 28k-holder figure and 112-day trading history initially suggest organic distribution, yet they sit in direct tension with the top-10 wallets holding the entire supply outside LP/burn addresses; this implies the broad holder count is mostly dust wallets or abandoned positions while control remains centralized. Ownership renunciation and the absence of mint/proxy functions remove the most common rug vectors, and the custom verified contract (not a factory clone) plus near-zero taxes further distance it from obvious scam templates. However, the unlocked liquidity pool of only $86 means the same concentrated entities could still drain what little remains at any moment, and the $0.01 daily volume confirms the market has already priced it as dead. Taken together, the picture is coherent only as a failed or abandoned micro-cap that escaped early detection but never achieved real liquidity or decentralization.

The rule-based score correctly flags the concentration and unlocked LP as decisive negatives, but it underweights the verified custom source and renounced ownership because those two facts together eliminate post-launch tampering even if the original deployer still controls the top wallets. It also overweights the raw holder count without adjusting for the fact that 28k addresses are compatible with extreme concentration only if most positions are economically meaningless.

Next step: pull the top-10 holder addresses and cross-reference their creation dates and any common funding sources against the contract deployer transaction to test whether the concentration is a single actor or dispersed early buyers.
