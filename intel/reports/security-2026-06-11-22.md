## Recommended Actions

1. **Confirm multisig + timelock** on every ACP/ERC-8183 admin, deployer, upgrade, and evaluator key. This single control would have blocked the majority of 2026's largest exploits.
2. **Audit upgradeability:** enumerate any UUPS/transparent proxies in VAPE contracts; freeze or timelock-gate upgrade paths (Wasabi pattern, Base).
3. **Verify contract ownership on deploy/migration** — assign owner atomically; the Renegade.fi unowned-contract flaw let anyone overwrite logic.
4. **Review Safe modules & hooks** for caller-trust bugs (`msg.sender` vs caller-supplied data) — the New Market Trading missing-`require` class.
5. **Enforce cross-chain settlement finality** for any Base↔Ethereum ACP job flows; reject single-verifier bridge assumptions.
6. **Pin and vet the build pipeline** — no auto-updating unaudited editor/npm dependencies on machines holding deploy keys.
