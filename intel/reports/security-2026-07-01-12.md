## VAPE Impact Assessment
- **ACP ERC-8183 Relevance:** No direct hit — but the npm supply-chain campaign is a direct threat to any agent/bot dev pipeline, including ACP tooling.
- **Base Chain Exposure:** No — none of this cycle's incidents touched Base custody or Base-native protocols.
- **Agent Tokenization Risk:** Indirect — trading-bot credential theft (npm) is exactly the attack surface autonomous agent wallets present.
- **Cross-Chain Bridge Impact:** Yes — Syscoin + Secret/Axelar reaffirm bridge proof-validation as a live class; validates VIRTUAL's CCIP migration thesis.

---

## Recommended Actions
1. Lock down agent/bot dependency supply chains — pin npm versions, audit new packages (stake-math et al.), and never store plaintext keys where a compromised dev laptop can reach them.
2. Treat bridge proof-validation as the #1 cross-chain audit target; forged/incomplete proofs drove three of five incidents this cycle.
3. Enforce hardware-backed key isolation for any hot wallet with mint/bridge-admin rights — Humanity's $36M was a laptop, not a contract bug.
