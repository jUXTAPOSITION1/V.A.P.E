## VAPE Impact Assessment
- **ACP ERC-8183 Relevance:** Yes — recurring theme is proof-validation/relay logic and admin-key compromise. Any ACP agent custody or cross-chain settlement path must treat off-chain signer keys (cf. Taiko SGX key on GitHub) as primary attack surface.

- **Agent Tokenization Risk:** Indirect — credential/key theft dominates 2026 losses; agent wallets (non-custodial) reduce but don't eliminate signer-key risk.
- **Cross-Chain Bridge Impact:** Yes — bridges remain the dominant loss vector. Avoid newly-launched or thinly-audited bridge routes.
