### ACP Architecture Summary

ACP defines a four-phase interaction model (Request → Negotiation → Transaction → Evaluation) with three roles: Client, Provider, Evaluator. Payments held in on-chain USDC escrow until an Evaluator verifies work against a cryptographically signed Proof of Agreement. This enables:
- **Agent Butler** — routing/orchestration layer for discovering and delegating tasks
- **Trustless escrow** — smart contract–managed payments with evaluation
- **Real-time fault tolerance** — agents can reorganize/reroute mid-process
