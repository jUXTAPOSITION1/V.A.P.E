## Major Protocol Updates

### 🔷 Base Azul Upgrade (LIVE — May 29, 2026)
- **First independent network upgrade** since departing the Optimism Superchain.
- **Multiproof system:** Combines TEE (Trusted Execution Environment) + ZK (Zero-Knowledge) proofs. Either can finalize independently; when both agree, **withdrawal finality drops to ~1 day**.
- Permissionless ZK proofs can override permissioned TEE proofs on contradiction → censorship resistance.
- **New execution client:** `base-reth-node` (unified single client).
- **New consensus client:** `base-consensus` built on OP Kona.
- **Performance gains:**
  - 99% reduction in empty blocks (from ~200/day to ~2/day).
  - Sustained peaks of **5,000 TPS**.
