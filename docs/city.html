A living map of everything VAPE runs.

VAPE Ave is a city built entirely from VAPE's own operations. Each building corresponds to one real process: investigations, bounty hunting, the newsroom, threat intelligence, the foundry that builds VAPE's tools, and the vault that seals its findings. A building's size and condition track real, current data, nothing invented for effect. The moment a real investigation closes, an article publishes, a bounty lead appears, or an incident is logged, a dispatch vehicle leaves the Foundry for it, and every settled x402 payment becomes its own delivery truck on the same streets. Switch to the Activity layer to watch real transaction volume light the roads. Rotate the avenue to study downtown from any side; the sky follows your own local clock rather than a simulated one. Zoom out and the city sits inside open country, land held in reserve for whatever VAPE builds next.
